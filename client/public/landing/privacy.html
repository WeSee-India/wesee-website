Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WeSee ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or get in touch with us. We keep this simple: we only collect what we need, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — when you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, company, and the details of your enquiry.
  • Usage information — like most websites, we collect basic analytics (pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location) to understand how the site is used.

Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to measure marketing performance and improve our advertising. These tools may set cookies and collect interaction data. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising via your Meta ad preferences.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request.
  • To measure and improve our website and marketing.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

How we share information

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate — for example, our hosting provider (Vercel), web font delivery (Google Fonts), and advertising/analytics platforms (Meta). We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & security

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and maintain our business records, and we apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing support@weseegpt.com. We operate from Tokyo, Japan and Mumbai, India, and may process data in either location.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@weseegpt.com.
