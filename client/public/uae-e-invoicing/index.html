What is e-invoicing

A digital invoice, reported to the FTA.

01

What changes

From 2027, PDF and paper invoices will no longer count for B2B in the UAE. Every invoice has to go out digitally through a Ministry of Finance accredited provider, on the Peppol network, linked to your accounting software and reported to the FTA.

02

Who it applies to

Both the buyer and the seller need a provider. The rules cover in-scope B2B invoices across the UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. B2C invoices are out of scope.

03

The penalty

Not having a system in place can bring a fine of AED 5,000 a month. The dates differ by the size of your business, so it helps to know which phase you fall into.

What to know now

Choosing a provider is the easy part.

The work that takes time

  • Choosing a provider is the easy part. Getting it connected is what takes time.
  • Integration takes time. Linking a provider to your accounting or ERP software is not a same-day job.
  • Wrong TRNs and messy customer data can block invoices. Customer records need to be clean before invoices will go through.
  • Credit notes need testing. They have to work properly before you go live, not after the first return.
  • Support after go-live matters most. This is when invoices, credit notes and data issues actually show up.
  • Leaving it late creates a rush. The weeks before the deadline are when everyone is trying to get connected at once.
  • Big buyers go live from January 2027. They will expect suppliers to send e-invoices. If you are not set up, invoices and payments can get held up.

FAQ

What is e-invoicing in the UAE?
From 2027, PDF and paper invoices will no longer count for B2B. Every invoice has to go out digitally through a Ministry of Finance accredited provider, on Peppol, linked to your accounting software and reported to the FTA. Both the buyer and the seller need a provider.
When do we have to be ready?
If your revenue is AED 50M or more, you go live on 1 January 2027. If you are under AED 50M, you need to appoint a provider by 31 March 2027 and be live by 1 July 2027. Government entities appoint by 31 March 2027 and go live on 1 October 2027.
What is the penalty?
Not having a system in place can bring a fine of AED 5,000 a month, under Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025. Always check the official MoF and FTA sources for the full rules.
Does this apply if we only sell to consumers?
B2C invoices are out of scope. If you also invoice other businesses, those B2B invoices are in scope and need to go through a provider.
We already use accounting software. Do we still need a provider?
Yes. Your accounting or ERP software still does the day-to-day work. An accredited provider sits between that software and the FTA, so invoices go out in the required digital format. The hard part is connecting the two and getting your customer data right.
